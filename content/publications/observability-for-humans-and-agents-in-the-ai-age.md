Nicolas Wörner and I explore how AI agents can help with both OpenTelemetry instrumentation and telemetry analysis. We discuss using skills and code reviews to improve instrumentation, then moving from interactive investigations to automated analysis workflows. Throughout, useful results depend on purposeful instrumentation, clear alerts, and access to reliable telemetry.

Published on Bronto and cross-posted to OllyGarden.
