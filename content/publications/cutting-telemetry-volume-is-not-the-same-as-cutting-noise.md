Reducing telemetry volume does not automatically improve signal-to-noise. This article explores the tradeoffs between removing redundant data, losing information needed for investigations, and enriching telemetry to make it more useful. It argues for measuring the questions teams can answer rather than focusing solely on bytes per day.
